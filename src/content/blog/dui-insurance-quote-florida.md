Requesting a DUI insurance quote in Florida is really just requesting an FR-44 quote — the industry term for what your policy actually needs to include after a DUI conviction. Here's exactly how the process works and what to expect.

## What a "DUI insurance quote" really means

There's no separate insurance category called "DUI insurance." What you're actually requesting is a standard auto policy built around FR-44's higher liability limits, shopped across carriers that write these policies. Understanding that distinction helps you ask for the right thing when you start comparing options.

## What you'll need on hand

- Your driver's license
- Vehicle details — year, make, model, and VIN if available
- Your court or FLHSMV notice, which typically confirms your specific requirement and dates
- Any prior insurance information, especially if you're switching carriers

## Why quotes vary so much after a DUI

This is where people often get frustrated — three quotes, three very different numbers, all for the same driver and vehicle. That's not a mistake. Every insurer weighs DUI-related risk differently, which means the carrier you choose can matter as much as your actual driving history in determining your final price.

## How fast you can actually get a quote

Usually within minutes during business hours. If you'd rather not fill out an online form, [Florida FR-44 Auto Insurance](/quote/) also takes quote requests by text — many people find this faster, especially if they're managing this alongside a lot of other stress.

## Quote comparison checklist

| Step | What to check |
|---|---|
| Coverage limits | Confirm they match your specific FR-44 requirement |
| Carrier eligibility | Confirm the insurer actually files FR-44 |
| Total cost | Compare full premium, not just a monthly teaser rate |
| Discounts | Ask what you qualify for — every carrier differs |
| Filing timeline | Confirm how quickly the FR-44 gets submitted once active |

## What happens after you accept a quote

Once you choose an option, you'll activate the policy and your insurer takes care of filing the FR-44 electronically. There's no separate DMV visit required on your end — the whole thing typically wraps up within a day or two of accepting a quote.

## What to do if you feel rushed

Court deadlines can create real time pressure, but that doesn't mean you have to accept the first quote out of panic. Even with a tight timeline, most drivers can still get two or three quotes within the same day or two — the process moves fast enough that comparing doesn't meaningfully cost you time, even under pressure.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is requesting a quote free?** Always, and there's no obligation to purchase.

**Will multiple quote requests hurt my credit?** Quote requests themselves generally don't involve a hard credit pull — ask your agent if you have concerns about a specific carrier's process.

**How many quotes should I compare?** At least two or three, given how much FR-44 pricing varies between insurers.

**Can I get a quote before my court date?** Yes — many drivers start shopping as soon as they know FR-44 will be required, even before every detail of their case is finalized.

**What if my quote seems too high?** Get a second and third opinion before accepting — the gap between the highest and lowest FR-44 quote for the same driver is often larger than people expect.
