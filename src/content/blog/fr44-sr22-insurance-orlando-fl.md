## Sorting out FR-44 versus SR-22

FR-44 is generally tied to a DUI or DWI conviction and requires liability coverage above Florida's standard minimums. SR-22 typically applies to situations like driving without insurance, at standard coverage limits. Check your court or FLHSMV notice, or see our [FR-44 vs. SR-22 comparison](/guides/fr44-vs-sr22/) for full details.
