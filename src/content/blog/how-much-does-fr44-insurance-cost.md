There's no single number that applies to every driver, but understanding what drives FR44 pricing can help you set realistic expectations — and shop smarter.

## Two forces push the price up

FR44 policies tend to cost more than standard auto insurance for two separate reasons. First, the required liability limits are higher than Florida's standard minimums, so you're simply buying more coverage. Second, the conviction behind the requirement (usually a DUI) signals higher risk to insurers, which affects your rate independent of the coverage limits.

## What actually moves your number

- **Driving record.** Additional violations, accidents, or a history of lapses all raise your rate.
- **Vehicle.** Higher-value or higher-performance vehicles typically cost more to insure.
- **Location.** Rates vary by ZIP code based on local claims data.
- **Carrier.** This is the big one — not every insurer prices FR44 risk the same way, and quotes can vary substantially between companies for the exact same driver.

## The single biggest lever: comparison shopping

Because FR44 pricing varies so much carrier to carrier, comparing multiple quotes is consistently the most effective way to lower your cost. A driver who only gets one quote has no way of knowing whether they're paying a competitive rate or a padded one.

## A few more ways to help your rate

Ask about paid-in-full discounts, defensive driving course credits, and bundling options. Keep your coverage continuous — even a short lapse can undo any progress you've made toward a lower rate over time.
