Getting an SR-22 in Florida is more straightforward than the paperwork makes it look. Here's the whole process, laid out step by step, so you know exactly what's coming next.

## Step 1: Confirm it's actually SR-22 you need

Check your court or FLHSMV notice — it should specifically say "SR-22." If it says "FR-44" instead, you're dealing with a different requirement that involves higher coverage limits. Our [FR-44 vs. SR-22 guide](/guides/fr44-vs-sr22/) can help you tell the two apart if the paperwork isn't clear.

## Step 2: Gather your information

- Driver's license
- Vehicle year, make, and model
- Your court or FLHSMV notice with your specific requirement
- Prior insurance info, if you're switching companies

## Step 4: Compare more than one quote

SR-22 pricing can vary meaningfully between carriers for the same driver. Two or three quotes gives you a real sense of the market instead of guessing based on one number.

## Step 5: Activate your policy

Once you choose a carrier, you'll set up your policy at Florida's standard minimum coverage levels and get it active — this is usually the trigger for the SR-22 filing.

## Step 6: Let your insurer handle the filing

You don't file anything yourself. Once your policy is active, your carrier submits the SR-22 electronically to the FLHSMV, typically without any extra steps on your end.

## Step 7: Maintain continuous coverage

This is the step that matters most long-term. Keep your policy active for your full required period — usually around three years — without any lapse.

## Setting yourself up for a smooth three years

Once your SR-22 is active, the hardest part is behind you — but the requirement doesn't end until your full filing period is complete. A few habits make that stretch a lot easier: set up autopay so a missed payment never becomes an accidental lapse, keep your address current with your insurer so renewal notices actually reach you, and mark your filing end date somewhere you'll actually see it. None of this is complicated, but it's exactly the kind of thing that's easy to let slip when life gets busy — and slipping here is the one mistake that actually costs you time and money.

## If your timeline is tight

Sometimes there's a court date or a reinstatement deadline bearing down, and "get it done eventually" isn't good enough. If that's your situation, say so upfront when you request quotes. Most SR-22 policies can be activated same-day once you've chosen a carrier, and being clear about your timeline up front helps your agent prioritize accordingly instead of guessing.

## Frequently asked questions

**How quickly can I get SR-22 coverage active?** Often within a day, sometimes faster, once you have your information ready.

**Do I need to visit the DMV myself?** No — your insurer's electronic filing handles the state-facing part of the process.

**Can I get SR-22 coverage without owning a vehicle?** Yes, non-owner SR-22 policies exist for exactly this situation.

**What if I'm switching from another state?** Confirm with your new Florida carrier whether your existing filing transfers or needs to be re-established under Florida's requirements.

**What's the biggest reason people delay getting SR-22 sorted out?** Usually it's just not knowing where to start. Once you have your notice and basic info in hand, the actual shopping and filing move quickly — the hardest part is often just getting started.
