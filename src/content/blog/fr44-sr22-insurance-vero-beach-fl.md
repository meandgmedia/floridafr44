Vero Beach drivers navigating an FR-44 or SR-22 requirement follow the same statewide rules as every other Florida driver, but getting it right quickly still depends on understanding your specific situation and finding a carrier that actually writes these filings.

## Figuring out which requirement applies to you

DUI and DWI convictions typically trigger FR-44, which requires liability coverage above Florida's standard minimums. Other situations — like driving without insurance or certain violations — more commonly trigger SR-22, at standard coverage limits. Check your court or FLHSMV notice, or see our [FR-44 vs. SR-22 comparison](/guides/fr44-vs-sr22/) for a full breakdown.
