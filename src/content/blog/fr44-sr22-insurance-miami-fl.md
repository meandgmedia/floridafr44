## Confirming FR-44 versus SR-22

FR-44 typically applies after a DUI or DWI conviction and requires liability coverage above Florida's standard minimums. SR-22 more commonly applies to situations like driving without insurance, at standard coverage limits. Your court or FLHSMV notice specifies which one applies — see our [FR-44 vs. SR-22 guide](/guides/fr44-vs-sr22/) for the complete breakdown.
