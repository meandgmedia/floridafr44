"Best" means something different for every driver shopping after a DUI. For some it's the lowest possible price. For others it's a carrier known for smooth claims handling, or an agent who actually returns calls. Here's how to define — and actually find — the best fit for your situation.

## Start by defining what "best" means to you

Before comparing quotes, get honest about your real priorities. Is price the only thing that matters, or would you pay a little more for a carrier known for better service? Do you want the flexibility to add comprehensive and collision coverage later, or is bare-minimum compliance the goal for now? There's no wrong answer, but knowing your own priorities makes comparing quotes far more useful.

## Price matters, but it isn't everything

The cheapest FR-44 quote isn't automatically the best one if it comes from a carrier with a track record of slow claims or poor communication. That said, price absolutely matters — the goal is finding genuine value, not just the lowest number on a screen.

## What separates a good FR-44 carrier from a mediocre one

- Clear communication about your specific filing requirement
- A track record of actually filing FR-44 certificates correctly and on time
- Reasonable claims handling if you ever need it
- Transparent pricing without hidden fees buried in the fine print
- Willingness to explain your policy in plain language, not jargon

## How to evaluate carriers beyond price

Ask direct questions: How long has this carrier written FR-44 policies? What's their process if you need to file a claim? Do they offer flexible payment options? A good agent should be able to answer these clearly, without dodging or redirecting back to price alone.

## Comparing what actually matters

| Factor | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Price | Obviously important, but not the only factor |
| FR-44 filing experience | Reduces risk of errors or delays |
| Claims reputation | Matters if you ever need to use your coverage |
| Communication style | Affects how smoothly your policy period goes |
| Discount availability | Can meaningfully offset an otherwise higher price |

## Trusting your gut, within reason

Numbers and comparisons matter, but so does how an agent or carrier makes you feel during the process — rushed and dismissed, or actually heard and informed. If something feels off during your shopping process, whether it's vague answers or pressure to decide immediately, that's worth paying attention to. The best insurance after a DUI usually comes with a straightforward, patient conversation, not a hard sell.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the cheapest quote ever actually the best choice?** Sometimes, yes — if the carrier is reputable and the coverage matches your requirement exactly. It's just not automatic.

**How do I know if a carrier is experienced with FR-44?** Ask directly, or work with an agency that already knows which carriers handle FR-44 filings well.

**Should I prioritize a local agent over an online-only insurer?** Both can work well — what matters more is responsiveness and experience with FR-44 specifically, regardless of format.

**Can "best" change over time?** Yes — what fits your situation right after a DUI might not be the best fit once your FR-44 period ends and your options broaden again.

**What's the most overlooked factor when people shop after a DUI?** Claims handling reputation. People focus heavily on price and forget to ask what happens if they actually need to file a claim during their FR-44 period.
