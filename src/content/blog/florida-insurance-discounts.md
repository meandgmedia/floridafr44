Even with a higher-risk filing like FR44 or SR22, there are usually discounts available that can help offset the cost. Here are the ones worth asking about.

## Multi-policy (bundling) discount

Combining your auto policy with homeowners or renters insurance through the same carrier often unlocks a meaningful discount on both policies.

## Paid-in-full discount

Paying your six- or twelve-month premium upfront, rather than monthly, frequently comes with a lower overall cost — and avoids monthly installment fees some carriers charge.

## Defensive driving course discount

Completing a state-approved defensive driving course can lower your premium and, depending on your situation, may also help with points on your license.

## Good driver / claims-free discount

A clean record without recent claims or violations (aside from the one that triggered your FR44 or SR22, if applicable) can qualify you for a better rate over time.

## Low-mileage discount

If you don't drive many miles annually, some carriers offer a discount for reduced exposure — worth asking about if you work from home or use public transit regularly.

## Autopay / electronic billing discount

Simple, but easy to miss: some insurers offer a small discount just for enrolling in automatic payments, which also helps you avoid an accidental lapse.
