Not every insurance company writes FR-44 policies. Some simply don't participate in high-risk filings at all, focusing instead on standard, low-risk drivers. This isn't personal — it's a business decision some carriers make, and it means your search needs to be targeted rather than general.

## What these carriers actually look like

FR-44-eligible carriers range from large national insurers with dedicated high-risk divisions to smaller companies that specialize almost entirely in non-standard risk. Neither type is inherently better — what matters is finding one that offers competitive pricing for your specific situation and has genuine experience filing FR-44 certificates correctly.

## Questions worth asking any carrier directly

- Do you write FR-44 policies in Florida specifically?
- How quickly do you typically file the certificate once a policy is active?
- What happens if I need to switch carriers mid-term — will the transition be smooth?
- Are there discounts available even for FR-44 policies?

## Carrier types at a glance

| Carrier type | Typical fit |

## Why this list changes over time

Which carriers actively write FR-44 policies isn't fixed forever — companies adjust their risk appetite based on broader market conditions, sometimes entering or exiting this space entirely. This is one more reason working with an agency that tracks these changes in real time beats relying on outdated information from a few years back, since a carrier that worked for a friend previously might not be taking new FR-44 business today.
