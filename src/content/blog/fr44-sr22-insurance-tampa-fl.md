Tampa drivers facing an FR-44 or SR-22 requirement are working within the same statewide Florida process as drivers anywhere else — knowing your specific situation and finding a carrier that actually writes these policies is what actually speeds things up.

## Determining FR-44 versus SR-22

FR-44 is typically tied to a DUI or DWI conviction and requires liability coverage above Florida's standard minimums. SR-22 more commonly applies to situations like driving without insurance, at standard coverage limits. Check your court or FLHSMV notice, or see our [FR-44 vs. SR-22 guide](/guides/fr44-vs-sr22/) for the full comparison.
