Whether you're on a standard policy or navigating an FR44 requirement, a handful of strategies consistently make the biggest difference in what you pay.

## 1. Compare multiple carriers

This is the single most effective step. Pricing for the same driver and vehicle can vary significantly from one insurer to the next, especially for higher-risk filings like FR44 and SR22. Getting quotes from several carriers — rather than accepting the first number you see — is how you find out whether you're actually getting a competitive rate.

## 2. Keep your coverage continuous

Lapses are expensive. A gap in coverage doesn't just risk a suspended license under FR44 or SR22 requirements — it also tends to push future premiums higher, since continuous coverage is one of the factors insurers weigh favorably.

## 3. Ask about every available discount

Bundling home or renters insurance with auto, paying in full instead of monthly, completing a defensive driving course, and low-mileage discounts can all add up. Not every carrier offers the same discounts, so it's worth asking directly.

## 4. Choose your vehicle with insurance in mind

A vehicle's value, repair cost, and safety rating all factor into your premium. If you're shopping for a car while under an FR44 or SR22 requirement, it's worth checking how a specific model affects your quote before you commit.

## 5. Drive clean going forward

Additional violations during a required filing period can increase your rate further and, in some cases, extend how long you need to carry the higher coverage. A clean record is the most reliable way to see your rate improve over time.
