Port St. Lucie drivers dealing with an FR-44 or SR-22 requirement have the same statewide rules as anyone else in Florida — but knowing exactly what applies to your situation, and finding a carrier that actually writes these policies, is where local guidance actually helps.

## FR-44 vs. SR-22: which one applies to you

If your requirement stems from a DUI or DWI conviction, you're most likely looking at FR-44, which requires liability coverage above Florida's standard minimums. If it's tied to driving without insurance or certain other violations, SR-22 — at standard coverage limits — is more common. Your court or FLHSMV notice will specify which one applies to your case.
