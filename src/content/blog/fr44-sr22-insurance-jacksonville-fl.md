## Sorting out FR-44 versus SR-22

FR-44 generally follows a DUI or DWI conviction and requires liability coverage above Florida's standard minimums. SR-22 more commonly applies to situations like driving without insurance, at standard coverage limits. Check your court or FLHSMV notice, or see our [FR-44 vs. SR-22 guide](/guides/fr44-vs-sr22/) for the complete comparison.
