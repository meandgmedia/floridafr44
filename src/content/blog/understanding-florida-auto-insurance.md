Florida's insurance rules are a little different from many other states, which can make shopping for a policy confusing if you're not familiar with the terminology. Here's a beginner-friendly overview.

## Required coverage

Florida requires two coverages for nearly all drivers: Personal Injury Protection (PIP), which covers your own medical costs after an accident regardless of fault, and Property Damage Liability (PDL), which covers damage you cause to someone else's property. Bodily injury liability becomes required in certain situations, including FR44 and SR22 cases.

## Optional coverage worth considering

Comprehensive and collision coverage protect your own vehicle — comprehensive for non-collision events like theft or weather damage, collision for accident damage. Uninsured/underinsured motorist coverage protects you if you're hit by a driver with inadequate insurance, which is common enough in Florida that many drivers add it.

## How rates are determined

Insurers weigh your driving record, vehicle, location, coverage limits, and — for FR44/SR22 cases — the underlying conviction. Because pricing methods vary by carrier, the same driver can see meaningfully different quotes from different insurers.

## Shopping smart

The most effective way to control your cost is comparing multiple carriers rather than renewing with the same insurer automatically each year. This matters even more for high-risk filings like FR44 and SR22, where pricing differences between carriers tend to be largest.
