## What an FR-44 quote actually involves

An FR-44 quote isn't some mysterious, separate product. It's a regular auto insurance quote — just built around the higher liability limits Florida requires after certain DUI-related convictions. When you request one, an agent (ideally one who does this every day, not once a year) checks your driving history, your vehicle, and your specific court or FLHSMV requirement, then shops that profile across carriers that actually write FR-44 policies.

That last part matters more than people realize. Not every insurer offers FR-44 filings, so a "quick quote" from a random comparison site sometimes disappears the moment you mention FR-44. Working with [Florida FR-44 Auto Insurance](/quote/) skips that dead end — we only shop carriers we already know write these policies.

## What you'll need before you start

Getting a fast, accurate quote comes down to having a few things handy:

- Your driver's license
- Vehicle info (year, make, model, VIN if you have it)
- Your court or FLHSMV notice, if you have one — it usually spells out your exact requirement
- Any prior insurance details, especially if you're switching companies

If you don't have all of this yet, don't stress. You can start the process with what you've got and fill in the rest.

## Why quotes vary so much between companies

Here's the part that surprises people: get three FR-44 quotes and you might see three wildly different numbers for the exact same car and driving record. That's not a scam — it's just how high-risk pricing works. Every insurer weighs your conviction, your coverage limits, and your overall risk profile a little differently.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is requesting a quote free?** Yes, always — no obligation to buy anything.

**Will getting a quote affect my credit?** A quote request itself typically doesn't involve a hard credit pull. Ask your agent directly if you're unsure about a specific carrier's process.
