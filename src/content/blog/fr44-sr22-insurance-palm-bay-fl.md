Palm Bay drivers dealing with an FR-44 or SR-22 requirement are working within the same statewide Florida rules as everyone else, but knowing your specific situation and finding the right carrier still takes some navigating.

## Determining which requirement fits your case

FR-44 is generally tied to DUI or DWI convictions and requires liability coverage above Florida's standard minimums. SR-22 typically applies to situations like driving without insurance or certain other violations, at standard coverage limits. Your court or FLHSMV notice will specify which applies — or see our [FR-44 vs. SR-22 guide](/guides/fr44-vs-sr22/) for a full comparison.
