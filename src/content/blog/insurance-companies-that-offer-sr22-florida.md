Finding out mid-quote that your preferred insurer doesn't actually offer SR-22 filings is one of the more common frustrations drivers run into. Here's how to identify carriers that genuinely work with SR-22 cases, and what to look for once you have.

## Why not every insurer offers this

SR-22 filings require insurers to take on specific reporting obligations to the state, and not every company participates. Some general insurers focus exclusively on standard, lower-risk policies and simply don't offer SR-22 as part of their business. This isn't a reflection of your situation — it's a business decision some carriers make.

## What to look for beyond just "do they offer it"

- **Filing speed.** How quickly does the carrier typically submit the SR-22 certificate once your policy is active?
- **Claims reputation.** If you ever need to use your coverage, how responsive is this carrier known to be?
- **Discount availability.** Does this carrier offer discounts that apply even to SR-22 policies?
- **Payment flexibility.** Are monthly payment plans available, and what fees apply?

## Questions worth asking directly

Before committing to a quote, it's reasonable to ask: How long have you been writing SR-22 policies in Florida? What's your typical filing turnaround time? What happens if I need to switch policies mid-term? A carrier's willingness to answer clearly is often a good sign of how smoothly your experience with them will go.

## Why this list shifts over time

Which carriers actively offer SR-22 filings isn't permanent — companies adjust their risk appetite based on broader market conditions, sometimes entering or exiting this segment of the business entirely. This is exactly why working with an agency that tracks these changes in real time serves you better than relying on outdated information about which companies used to offer this filing.
