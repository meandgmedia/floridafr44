"Cheap" and "FR-44 insurance" don't usually show up in the same sentence. Required coverage limits are higher than standard, and the conviction behind your filing pushes the price up too. But here's the thing — cheap is relative, and there's a real, meaningful gap between what you *could* pay and what you might end up paying if you shop lazily.

## Why FR-44 costs more in the first place

Quick refresher: FR-44 requires higher liability limits than a standard Florida policy, and it's typically triggered by a DUI or DWI conviction. Insurers price both of those factors in. That's simply the starting line — but where you finish depends heavily on how you shop.

## The single biggest lever: comparing carriers

If you only do one thing to lower your FR-44 rate, make it this: get quotes from more than one carrier. Seriously. FR-44 pricing swings more between insurers than almost any other type of policy, because each company weighs DUI-related risk with its own formula. One carrier's "high-risk surcharge" might be another's "pretty standard case."

## Other ways to trim the number

- **Pay in full if you can.** Monthly plans often carry installment fees that add up over a policy term.
- **Ask about every discount.** Defensive driving courses, bundling, and autopay discounts all stack.
- **Keep coverage continuous.** A lapse doesn't just risk your license — it also tends to push future rates higher.
- **Pick your vehicle carefully.** A high-value or high-performance car adds cost on top of your FR-44 surcharge.
- **Stay clean going forward.** Additional violations during your filing period can push your rate up further and even extend how long you're required to carry FR-44.

## A quick reality check

| Approach | Typical impact on rate |
|---|---|
| Getting only one quote | Highest risk of overpaying |
| Comparing 3+ carriers | Usually the biggest single savings |
| Paying in full vs. monthly | Small-to-moderate savings |
| Bundling with home/renters | Moderate savings, varies by carrier |
| Clean record going forward | Improves rate over time |

## Frequently asked questions

**Is there a carrier that's always cheapest for FR-44?** No — pricing shifts based on your specific driving history, vehicle, and location, which is exactly why comparing matters more than chasing one "best" company.

**Does a higher deductible lower my FR-44 premium?** It can, for the comprehensive and collision portions of your policy, though your FR-44-required liability limits stay fixed regardless.

**Can I lower my rate mid-policy?** Sometimes, especially if a new discount becomes available or your situation changes. It's worth asking your agent periodically.

**Will my FR-44 rate ever go back to normal?** Once your required filing period ends — typically three years — you're generally free to shop for a standard policy again, often at a noticeably better rate.

## Bottom line

Cheap FR-44 insurance in Florida isn't a myth — it's just a byproduct of shopping smart. Compare multiple carriers, grab every discount you qualify for, and keep your record clean while you ride out your filing period.
