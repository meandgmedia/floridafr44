FR-44 coverage costs more than a standard policy, but the amount more varies a lot depending on how you shop for it. Here are the strategies that make the biggest difference, in order of impact.

## 1. Compare multiple carriers (the biggest lever)

FR-44 pricing varies more between carriers than standard auto insurance pricing does, because insurers weigh DUI-related risk differently. A driver who gets quotes from several carriers routinely finds a meaningfully lower rate than one who accepts the first quote they see. This single step usually has more impact than any discount.

## 2. Never let coverage lapse

A lapse doesn't just risk suspension and a restarted filing period — it also tends to push future premiums higher across the board, since continuous coverage is a factor insurers weigh favorably.

## 3. Pay in full when possible

Many carriers offer a discount for paying your six- or twelve-month premium upfront rather than monthly, and monthly plans sometimes carry installment fees that add up over a policy term.

## 4. Take a defensive driving course

Completing a state-approved course can lower your premium with some carriers, and may help address points on your license depending on your situation.

## 5. Choose your vehicle carefully

A vehicle's value, safety rating, and typical repair costs all factor into your premium. If you're shopping for a car while under an FR-44 requirement, checking how a specific model affects your quote before buying can save meaningfully over the filing period.

## 6. Bundle where it makes sense

If you have homeowners or renters insurance, bundling it with your auto policy through the same carrier can unlock a multi-policy discount — worth asking about even if your current insurer doesn't offer FR-44 filings, since you could bundle with a different one that does.

## 7. Keep your record clean going forward

Additional violations during your FR-44 period can raise your rate further and, in some cases, extend your required filing period. A clean record is the most reliable way to see improvement over time.

## 8. Revisit your quote periodically

If you're locked into a required filing period, that doesn't mean you're locked into one carrier. Comparing rates again partway through your term — especially if your initial quote was rushed — can reveal savings you missed the first time.
